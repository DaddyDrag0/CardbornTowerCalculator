How floors work

HP: floor(200 × 1.12floor − 1 × boss modifier), minimum 1. On every 10th floor, the boss modifier is 1.3. Damage: floor(HP / 2), minimum 1.

Enemy lineup: selected from the 128 regular Battle Tower floors using the weekly seed, with a ±3-floor offset through floor 128. Beyond floor 128, the seed picks from the last 33 tower lineups. The exact lineup is not inferred by this page.

Variants: Shiny on floors 1–24, Awakened 25–49, Fabled 50–74, Fabled Corrupted 75–99, Void 100–149, and Fabled Void from floor 150 onward.

Reset note: the game's client module uses a fixed 604,800-second weekly interval anchored at 05:00 UTC. The announcement describes Sunday midnight Central Time; the fixed UTC anchor can shift local clock time when daylight saving time changes.
